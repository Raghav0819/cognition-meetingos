User Journey
From Meeting to Action — End to End
1

Record on Google Meet via Chrome Extension

The Cognition extension runs silently during the meeting, capturing live speech with speaker labels. When done, the PM hits "Send to AI Agents". Company ID links the meeting to the right workspace. Transcript can also be pasted manually.

2

7-Agent CrewAI Pipeline Runs in Background

FastAPI triggers the async pipeline. Extraction → Assignment → Confidence → Validation → Follow-up → Audit → Summary agents execute sequentially. Tasks and MoM are written to Firestore. Average processing time: 30–60 seconds.

3

PM Reviews & Validates AI-Extracted Tasks

The PM Dashboard shows a live meeting card. On click: tasks with confidence scores, approve/reject buttons, the full AI-generated Minutes of Meeting, and a complete agent audit trail. One-tap approval or edit.

4

Employees See Their Tasks Automatically

Each employee's dashboard shows only their tasks — assigned by name from the transcript. They mark tasks done, overdue, or escalate. Overdue detection runs automatically on login.

5

Manager Tracks Team Performance in Real Time

A live analytics dashboard shows task distribution (donut chart), team completion rates (ranked progress bars), department meeting breakdown, and escalation alerts.
